## Product work

- **[Offscript](https://thetribeland.com/)** — I designed and built a speaking coach that turns a recording into feedback a speaker can use in the next attempt. My work covers the practice modes, recording and transcription, feedback and history, and the accounts and plans behind the live product.
- **Stocka** · In development — An Android-first sales and inventory app for Ghanaian retailers. I built the offline sales flow so a sale and its stock changes are saved together on the device. When the connection returns, the app can retry syncing without recording the sale twice.
- **Fleet operations platform** · In development — I worked across the management web app and the driver app, including fleet assignments, trips, and maintenance. I implemented permissions that limit what staff can see and change, and tested the handoffs from service work through cost review.
- **Project Ananse** · In development — A web and mobile learning platform for Ghana's curriculum. My work includes lesson authoring and review, learner and guardian access, and delivering lesson files only to users with permission. The current development environment uses synthetic data.
- **[IHearVoices](https://github.com/YoPerry007/IHearVoices-Ecommerce---YouTube)** — A voice-enabled mobile marketplace. I built customer, seller, and admin workflows around a shared checkout that gives each seller access to their own portion of an order, with Paystack payment verification on the server.
- **[GSE Corporate Actions Crawler](https://github.com/YoPerry007/gse-corporate-actions-crawler)** — A Python service that turns Ghana Stock Exchange announcements into dividend and meeting records. It avoids duplicates on repeat runs and flags incomplete records for review.
